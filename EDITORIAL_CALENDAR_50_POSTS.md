1. **ClearSERP Snippet Targeting**: Every post begins with a 50–75 word direct answer snippet that directly matches the target search query for position zero featured snippet captures.
2. **Biochemical Entities**: Inclusion of primary and secondary biochemical entities (SCFA ratios, incretins, bacterial species, enzymes, and tight-junction proteins).
3. **Structured Data Schemas**: Complete JSON-LD blocks for `Article`, `BreadcrumbList`, and `Recipe` (where applicable) on every post.
4. **Responsive 16:9 Image Assets**: WebP and JPG fallbacks generated across 7 standard breakpoints (1200x675, 1024x572, 768x429, 600x400, 500x500, 300x167, 150x150) matching authentic daylight editorial photography.
5. **YMYL Compliance**: Mandatory inclusion of the standardized Huliate medical disclaimer box (`huliate-ymyl-disclaimer`) on every article page before the author box and footer.
6. **Internal Link Siloing**: Strategic bidirectional internal linking between related articles, high-converting lead magnets (`/35g-daily-fiber-blueprint/`), and category hubs.
